**DEF**)
If an [[Extension field|extension field]] $E$ of a field $F$ is of [[Finite dimensional|finite dimension]] $n$ as a [[Vector space|vector space]] over $F$, then $E$ is a **finite extension of degree $n$ over $F$**.
We shall let $[E:F]$ be the degree $n$ of $E$ over $F$.

___

To say that a field $E$ is a finite extension of a field $F$ does *not* mean that $E$ is a finite field.
It just asserts that $E$ is a finite-dimensional vector space over $F$, that is, that $[E:F]$ is finite.

We shall often use the fact that if $E$ is a finite extension of $F$, then, $[E:F] = 1 \Leftrightarrow E = F$.
[[Thm 30.19]] says that $\{1\}$ can be always be enlarged to a basis for $E$ over $F$.
Thus $[E:F] = 1 \iff E = F(1) = F$. ([[Simple extension]]) ^1c87a8
