##### Problem 1
For $\mathbf{E}=E_{0}te^{-t^{2}}\mathbf{a}_{z}$ in free space, find the displacement current crossing an area of $0.1[\text{m}^{2}]$ in the $xy$-plane from the $-z$ side to the $+z$ side at $t=1$.
___
Sol)
Note that $\mathbf{D}$ in free space is $\mathbf{D}= \varepsilon_{0}\mathbf{E}$.
So,
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dt} \int \mathbf{D}\cdot \, d\mathbf{S} &= \frac{d}{dt} 0.1\varepsilon_{0}E_{0}te^{-t^{2}}\\
&= 0.1\varepsilon_{0}E_{0}(e^{-t^{2}}-2t^{2}e^{-t^{2}}) \\
&=0.1\varepsilon_{0}E_{0}(1-2t^{2})e^{-t^{2}} \\
&=\boxed{ -0.1\varepsilon_{0}e^{-1}E_{0}[\text{A}] } \text{ at }t=1
\end{align}
$$
