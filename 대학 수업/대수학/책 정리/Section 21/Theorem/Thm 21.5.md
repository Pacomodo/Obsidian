**THEOREM**)
Any [[Integral Domain|integral domain]] $D$ can be enlarged to (or embedded in) a [[Field|field]] $F$ such that every element of $F$ can be expressed as a quotient of two elements of $D$.
Such a field $F$ is called [[Field of quotients of integral domain|a field of quotient of D]].
