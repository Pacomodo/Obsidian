___

**DEF**)
An **integral domain** $D$ is a commutative [[Ring|ring]] with unity $1 \neq 0$ and containing no [[Divisors of 0|0 divisors]].

___

**EXAMPLE**)

* $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_p$ for any prime $p$ are integral domains.
However, $\mathbb{Z}_n$ is not an integral domain if $n$ is not prime.

* The direct product $R \times S$ of two nonzero rings are not an integral domain.
Observe that for $r \in R$ and $s \in S$ both nonzero, but $(r, 0)(0,s) = (0,0)$.

* $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is an integral domain, but the matrix ring $M_2 (\mathbb{Z}_2 )$ is not.
Observe that $\begin{pmatrix}1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \\ \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \\ \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0&0 \\ \end{pmatrix}$.
