**THEOREM**)
$R$ is commutative [[Ring|ring]] with unity.
Every [[Maximal ideal|maximal ideal]] in $R$ is a [[Prime ideal|prime ideal]].

___

**PROOF**)
Let $M$ is a maximal ideal in R. then, by [[Thm 27.9]], $R/M$ is a field. Therefore, $R/M$ is an integral domain by [[Thm 19.9]]. Hence, $M$ is a prime ideal by [[Thm 27.15]].
