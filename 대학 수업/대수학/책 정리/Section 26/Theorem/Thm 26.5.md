**THEOREM**) (Analogue of  [[Thm 13.15]])
Let $\phi : R \rightarrow R'$ is a [[Ring homomorphism]]. Let $H$ is [[대학 수업/대수학/책 정리/Section 26/Definition/Kernel|Kernel]] of $\phi$.
Let $a \in R$. Then $\phi ^ {-1} [\phi (a)] = a + H = H + a$, where $a + H = H + a$ is the left and right [[Coset]] of the commutative [[Group]] $\left< H, + \right>$.
