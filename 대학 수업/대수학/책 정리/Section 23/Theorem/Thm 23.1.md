**THEOREM**) **(Division Algorithm for $F[x]$)**
Let $$f(x) = a_nx^n + \cdots + a_0$$ and $$g(x) = b_mx^m + \cdots + b_0$$ be two elements of $F[x]$ with $a_n, b_m \in F - \{0\}$ and $m>0$.
Then, there are unique polynomials $q(x)$ and $r(x)$ in $F[x]$ such that $f(x) = g(x)q(x) + r(x)$, where either $r(x) = 0$ or $deg(r(x)) < m$.
