**REMARK**: The integer ring $\mathbb{Z}$ and the polynomial ring $F[x]$ over field $F$ share a few common properties.
1. They are integral domain.
2. There are division algorithms for $\mathbb{Z}$ and for $F[x]$.
3. $\mathbb{Z}$ and $F[x]$ are PID.
4. $\mathbb{Z}$ and $F[x]$ are UFD.

___

In this section, we will see that if an integral domain $D$ has a division algorithm, then it is a PID and hence a UFD.

Go to [[Thm 46.4]].

To understand this theorem, we begin with what is a division algorithm for an arbitrary integral domain $D$.
