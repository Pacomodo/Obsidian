**THEOREM**)
Let $R$ be a [[Ring|ring]] with unity.
If $n \cdot 1_R \neq 0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, then $R$ has [[Characteristic|characteristic]] $0$.
(If $|1_R|$ is infinite, then $char(R) = 0$.)
If $n \cdot 1_R = 0$ for some $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, then the smallest such integer $n$ is the characteristic of $R$.
(If $1_R$ has a finite order as an element of the group $(R, +)$, then $char(R) = |1_R|$.)
___

**PROOF**)
If $n \cdot 1 \neq 0$ for all $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, then there is no $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ such that $n \cdot a = 0$ for all $a \in R$.
If $n \cdot 1 = 0$, then, for any $a \in R$, $n\cdot a = a + a + \cdots a = a(1+1+\cdots + 1) = a(n\cdot 1) = a\cdot 0 = 0$.
