Give an example to show that a [[Factor ring|factor ring]] of a [[Ring|ring]] with [[Divisors of 0|divisors of 0]] may be an integral domain.

___

**SOL**)
Consider $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$. This is not an [[Integral Domain|integral domain]].
Consider $(\mathbb{Z} \times \{0\})$, which is [[Ideal|ideal]] of $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$.
Consider a [[Factor ring|factor ring]], $(\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}) / (\mathbb{Z} \times \{0\} ) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.
This is an integral domain.

Consider $\mathbb{Z}_6$. This is not an integral domain.
Consider an ideal of $\mathbb{Z}_6$, $\{ 0. 3\}$.
Then, $\mathbb{Z}_6 / \{0,3\} \cong \mathbb{Z}_3$, where integral domain.
