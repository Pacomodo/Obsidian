**DEF**) Let $R$ be a commutative [[Ring|ring]] with unity.
Two elements $a, b \in R$ are **associates in** $R$ if $a = bu$ where $u \in R$ is a [[Unit|unit]] in $R$.

___

This relation is equivalence relation in $R$.
Go to [[대학 수업/대수학/책 정리/Section 45/Exercise/Exercise 27]].

___

**EXAMPLES**)

* The only units in $\mathbb{Z}$ are $1$ and $-1$. Thus the only associates of $n$ in $\mathbb{Z}$ are $n$ and $-n$ for positive integer $n$.
