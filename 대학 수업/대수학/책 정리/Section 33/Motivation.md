The purpose of this section is to determine the structure of all finite fields. (왜?)
We want to show that for **every** prime $p$ and positive integer $n$, there is **exactly one** finite field (up to isomorphism) of order $p^n$.
This field $\mathrm{GF}(p^n)$ is usually referred to as the **Galois field of order $p^n$**.
We shall be using a quite bit of our material on cyclic groups.
The proofs are simple and elegant.
