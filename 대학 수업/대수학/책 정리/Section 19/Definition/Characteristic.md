___

**DEF**)
For a [[Ring|ring]] $R$, a positive integer $n$ exists such that $n \cdot a$ for $a \in R$, then the least such positive integer $n$ is **the characteristic of the ring $R$**.
If there is no such positive integer $n$, we say $R$ is of characteristic $0$.

___

**EXAMPLE**)

* The ring $\mathbb{Z}_n$ is of characteristic $n$, while $\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R},$ and $\mathbb{C}$ all have characteristic $0$.
