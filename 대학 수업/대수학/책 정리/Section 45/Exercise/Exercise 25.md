Prove that if $p$ is a [[Prime|prime]] in an [[Integral Domain|integral domain]] $D$, then $p$ is an [[Irreducible|irreducible]].

___

**SOL**)
We want to show that if $p = ab$ where $a, b \in D$, then, either $a$ or $b$ is a unit.
Suppose that $p = ab$ and $p$ is a prime.
Since $p$ divides $ab$, either $p$ divides $a$ or $p$ divides $b$.
If $p$ divides $a$, then $pc = a$ for some $c \in D$. So, $p = ab = pcb \implies 1 = cb$, so, $b$ is unit.
If $p$ divides $b$, then $pc = b$ for some $c \in D$. So, $p = ab = apc = pac \implies 1 = ac$, so, $a$ is unit.
