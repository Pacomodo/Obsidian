___

**LEMMA**)
$\mathbb{Z}[i]$ is an [[Integral Domain]].

___

**PROOF**)
It is obvious that $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ is commutative ring with unity.
We want to show that there are no zero divisors.
Let $\alpha \beta = 0$, then by [[Lem 47.2]], $N(\alpha)N(\beta) = N(\alpha \beta) = N(0) = 0$.
So, $N(\alpha) = 0$ or $N(\beta) = 0$.
So, $\alpha = 0$ or $\beta = 0$.
