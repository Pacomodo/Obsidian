**THEOREM**) **(First Isomorphism Theorem)**
Let $\phi : G \rightarrow G'$ be a homomorphism with kernel $K$, let $\gamma_K : G \rightarrow G/K$ be the canonical homomorphism.
There is a unique isomorphism $\mu : G/K \rightarrow \phi[G]$ such that $\phi(x) = \mu(\gamma_K(x))$ for each $x \in G$.
