___

**THEOREM**)
The [[Characteristic|characteristic]] of a [[Field]] $F$ is either prime or $0$.

___

**PROOF**)
If $char(F) \neq 0$, by [[Cor 27.18]], $F$ contains $\mathbb{Z}_n$ as a subring. Then, $n$ must be prime, otherwise, $F$ contains [[Divisors of 0|0 divisor]]. Since $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a field by [[Cor 19.12]], $\mathbb{Z}_p \leq F$.
If $char(F) = 0$, by [[Cor 27.18]], $F$ contains $\mathbb{Z}$ as a subring. Then, by [[Cor 21.8]] and [[Cor 21.9]], $F$ must contain a [[Field of quotients of integral domain|field of quotients]] of $\mathbb{Z}$, which is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}$.

___

==Thus, every field $F$ contains a subfield isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$ or a subfield isomorphic to $\mathbb{Q}$.==
These $\mathbb{Z}_p$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ are the **fundamental building blocks** on which all fields rest.
