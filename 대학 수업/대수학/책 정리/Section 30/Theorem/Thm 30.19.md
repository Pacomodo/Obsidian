**THEOREM**)
Let $S = \{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \cdots, \alpha_r\}$ be a finite set of [[Linearly dependent, independent|linearly independent]] vectors of a [[Finite dimensional|finite dimensional]] [[Vector space|vector space]] $V$ over a field $F$.
Then $S$ can be enlarged to a [[Basis|basis]] for $V$ over $F$. Furthermore, if $B = \{\beta_1, \beta_2, \cdots, \beta_n\}$ is any basis for $V$ over $F$, then $r \leq n$.
