**DEF**)
By multiplying by a suitable constant in $F$, we can assume that the coeffiecient of the highest power of $x$ appearing in $p(x)$ of [[Thm 29.13]] is $1$.
Such a polynomial having $1$ as the coefficient of the highest power of the $x$ appearing is a **monic polynomial**.
A monic polynomial can be written as $$x^n + c_{n-1}x^{n-1} + \cdots + c_1x + c_0$$
