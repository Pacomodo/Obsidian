___

**THEOREM**)
If $R$ is a [[Ring|ring]] with unity, and $N$ is an [[Ideal|ideal]] of $R$ containing a [[Unit|unit]], then $N = R$.

___

**PROOF**)
Let $u \in N$ where $u$ is unit in $R$.
Since $N$ is an ideal of $R$, $rN \subseteq N$ for all $r \in R$.
Take $r = u^{-1} \in R$ and $u \in N$. then $1 \in N$.
Then, for any $r \in R$, $r\cdot 1 = r \in N$. So, $N = R$. (It means that if there is unity in $N$, then, the ideal $N$ is not proper ideal.)
