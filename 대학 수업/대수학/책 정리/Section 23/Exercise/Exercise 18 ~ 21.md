In Exercises 18 through 21, determine whether the polynomial in $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ satisfies an [[Thm 23.15|Eisenstein criterion]] for irreducibility over $\mathbb{Q}$.

**18**. $x^2 - 12$
**SOL**) Take $p = 3$. It is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$.
**19**. $8x^3+6x^2-9x+24$
**SOL**) Take $p = 3$. It is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$.
**20**. $4x^{10}-9x^3+24x-18$
**SOL**) No.
**21**. $2x^{10}-25x^3+10x^2-30$
**SOL**) Take $p = 5$. It is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$.
