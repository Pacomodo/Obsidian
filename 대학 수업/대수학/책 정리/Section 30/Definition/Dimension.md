___

**DEF**)
If $V$ is a [[Finite dimensional|finite dimensional]] [[Vector space|vector space]] over a [[Field|field]] $F$, the number of elements in a [[Basis|basis]] is the **dimension of $V$ over $F$**.
It is independent of the choice of basis. Go to [[Cor 30.20]].

___

**EXAMPLE**)

* Let $E$ be an [[Extension field|extension field]] of a field $F$. Let $\alpha \in E$. ^5b7497

[[Linearly dependent, independent#^1e73df|Example 30.14]] shows that if $\alpha$ is [[Algebraic, transcendental|algebraic]] over $F$ and $\deg(\alpha, F) = n$, then the dimension of $F(\alpha)$ as a vector space over $F$ is $n$.
**This is the important example**.
