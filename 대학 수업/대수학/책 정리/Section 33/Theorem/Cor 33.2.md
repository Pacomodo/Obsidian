**THEOREM**)
If $E$ is a finite field of characteristic $p$, then $E$ contains exactly $p^n$ elements for some positive integer $n$.

___

**PROOF**)
Every finite field $E$ is a [[Finite extension]] of a [[Prime field]], which is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$, where $p$ is the [[Characteristic]] of $E$.([[Thm 27.19]])
Hence, by [[Thm 33.1]], it proved.
