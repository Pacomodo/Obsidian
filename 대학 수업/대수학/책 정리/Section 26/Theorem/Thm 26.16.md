**THEOREM**) (Analogue of [[Thm 14.9]])
Let $N$ be an [[Ideal|ideal]] of a [[Ring|ring]] $R$. Then, $\gamma : R \rightarrow R/N$ where $x \mapsto (x+N)$ is a [[Ring homomorphism|ring homomorphism]] with [[대학 수업/대수학/책 정리/Section 26/Definition/Kernel|kernel]] $N$.

___

**PROOF**)
Additive part is done in [[Thm 14.9]].
So, it is enough to show that this map has homomorphism property in multiplication.
$$\gamma(ab) = ab + N = (a+N)(b+N) = \gamma(a)\gamma(b)$$
