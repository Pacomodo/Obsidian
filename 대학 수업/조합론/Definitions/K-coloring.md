___

**DEF**) A [[Coloring]] using at most $K$ colors is called a (proper) $K$-coloring.

___

Using the concepts of [[Color class]], we can also say that $K$-coloring is a partitioning of $V$ into $K$ color classes.

It means, $V = S_1 \cup S_2 \cup \cdots \cup S_K$ where $S_i \cap S_j = \varnothing$ $(i \neq j)$.

$S_i$ are all color class, also, [[Independent set]].

So, we can say $K$-coloring is a partition of $V$ into $K$-independent sets, so, we can call as $K$-partite.
