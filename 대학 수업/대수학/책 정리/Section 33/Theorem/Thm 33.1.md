___

**THEOREM**)
Let $E$ be a [[Finite extension|finite extension]] of degree $n$ over a finite field $F$.
If $F$ has $q$ elements, then $E$ has $q^n$ elements.

___

**PROOF**)
Let $\{\alpha_1, \cdots, \alpha_n\}$ be a basis for $E$ as a [[Vector space|vector space]] over $F$.
By [[Exercise 21|Exercise 21 of Section 30]], every $\beta \in E$ can be *uniquely* expressed in the form $$\beta = b_1\alpha_1 + b_2\alpha_2 + \cdots + b_n\alpha_n$$for $b_i \in F$.
Since each $b_i$ may be any of the $q$ elements of $F$, the total number of such distinct linear combinations of the $\alpha_i$ is $q^n$.
