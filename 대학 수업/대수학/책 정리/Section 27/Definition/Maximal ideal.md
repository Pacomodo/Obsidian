___

**DEF**)
A **maximal ideal of a ring $R$** is [[Ideal|ideal]] $M \neq R$ such that there is no ideal $N$ where $M \subset N \subset R$.

___

**EXAMPLES**)

* Consider $\mathbb{Z} / p\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$ where $p$ is a prime.
Let's put it off the multiplication part for a while.
We know that $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a [[Simple group|simple group]] for the additive part because $p$ is prime, so, $p\mathbb{Z}$ must be maximal normal subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}$ by [[Thm 15.18]].
Similarly, $p\mathbb{Z}$ is an [[Ideal|ideal]] of the [[Ring|ring]] $\mathbb{Z}$, moreover, $p\mathbb{Z}$ is maximal ideal of $\mathbb{Z}$. $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$ is a [[Field|field]].(Recall that [[Cor 27.6]])
Go to [[Thm 27.9]].
