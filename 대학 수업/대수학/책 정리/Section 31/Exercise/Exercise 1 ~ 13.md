In Exercises 1 through 13, find the degree and a basis for the given field extension.
Be prepared to justify your answers.

1. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

$irr(\sqrt{2}, \mathbb{Q}) = x^2 - 2$, so, $\deg$ is $2$ and a basis is $\{1, \sqrt{2}\}$.

2. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

basis is $\{1, \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{6}\}$

3. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{18})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

basis is $\{1, \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{6}\}$.

4. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt{3})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

basis is $\{1, 2^{1/3}, 2^{2/3}, 3^{1/2}, 2^{1/3}3^{1/2}, 2^{2/3}3^{1/2}\}$

5. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt[3]{2})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

basis is $\{1, 2^{1/2}, 2^{1/3}, 2^{2/3}, 2^{5/6}, 2^{1/6}\}$

6. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

$\deg(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}, \mathbb{Q}) = 4$, basis is $\{1, \sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{6}\}$.

7. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}\sqrt{3})$ over $\mathbb{Q}$

basis is $\{1, \sqrt{6}\}$

10. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{6})$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$

basis is $\{1,\sqrt{2}\}$

13. $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{6}+\sqrt{10})$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5})$

$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{5}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5})$
$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{6}+\sqrt{10}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{6}, \sqrt{10}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5})$
basis is $\{1, \sqrt{2}\}$.
