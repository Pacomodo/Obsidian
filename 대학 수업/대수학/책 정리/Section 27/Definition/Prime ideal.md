  ___

**DEF**)
An ideal $N \neq R$ in a commutative ring $R$ is a **prime ideal** if $ab \in N$ then either $a \in N$ or $b \in N$ for $a, b \in R$. (It means that $R/N$ has no [[Divisors of 0|zero divisor]].)

___

**EXAMPLE**)

* Note that $\{0\}$ is a obviously prime ideal.
* $\mathbb{Z} \times \{0\}$ is a prime ideal of $\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$.
If $(a, b)(c, d) \in \mathbb{Z} \times \{0\}$, then $bd = 0$, so, either $b = 0$ or $d = 0$.
