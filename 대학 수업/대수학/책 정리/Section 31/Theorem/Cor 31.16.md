___

**THEOREM**)
An [[Algebraically closed]] field $F$ has no proper [[Algebraic extension]].
That is, no algebraic extension $E$ with $F < E$.

___

**PROOF**)
Let $E$ be an algebraic extension of $F$, so $F \leq E$.
Then if $\alpha \in E$, there is $f(x) \in F[x]$ such that $f(\alpha) = 0$.
$f(x)$ can be factorized into linear factors by [[Thm 31.15]], so, there must be $(x-\alpha)$ in $f(x)$.
so, $\alpha \in F$. Thus $F = E$.
