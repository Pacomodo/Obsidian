Give an example to show that a [[Factor ring|factor ring]] of an [[Integral Domain|integral domain]] may have [[Divisors of 0|divisors of 0]].

___

**SOL**)
Consider an integral domain $\mathbb{Z}$.
Consider a factor ring $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ where $n$ is not prime.
$\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}_n$ have divisors of 0.
For example, $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$ have $3\cdot 4 = 0$.
