**LEMMA**)
In $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, the following properies of the norm function $N$ holds for all $\alpha , \beta \in \mathbb{Z}[i]$.

1. $N(\alpha) \geq 0$.
2. $N(\alpha) = 0 \Leftrightarrow \alpha = 0$.
3. $N(\alpha \beta) = N(\alpha) N(\beta)$.
