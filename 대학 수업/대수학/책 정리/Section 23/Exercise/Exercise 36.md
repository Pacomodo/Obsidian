(Remainder Theorem)
Let $f(x) \in F[x]$ where $F$ is a field, and let $\alpha \in F$.
Show that the remainder $r(x)$ when $f(x)$ is divided by $x-\alpha$, inaccordance with the division algorithm, is $f(\alpha)$.

___

**SOL**)
According to [[Thm 23.1|division algorithm]], $f(x) = (x-\alpha)g(x) + r(x)$ where $\deg(r(x)) < \deg(x-\alpha) = 1$, which is just constant in $F$. Let $r(x)$ denote $r \in F$.
Since $(x-\alpha)g(x)$ has $\alpha$ as zero, $f(\alpha) = 0 + r = r$.
