**LEMMA**) (Generalization of [[Thm 27.27]])
In a [[Principal ideal domain|PID]], if an [[Irreducible|irreducible]] $p | ab$, then either $p | a$ or $p | b$.

___

**PROOF**)
Let $D$ is a principal ideal domain, $p \in D$ and $p$ divides $ab$.
Then, $pc = (ab)$ for some $c \in D$, So, $(ab) \in \left< p \right>$. By [[Thm 45.12]], $\left< p \right>$ is [[Maximal ideal|maximal ideal]].
By [[Cor 27.16]], every maximal ideal is [[Prime ideal|prime ideal]], So, either $a \in \left< p \right>$ or $b \in \left< p \right>$.
Hence, either $p | a$ or $p | b$.
