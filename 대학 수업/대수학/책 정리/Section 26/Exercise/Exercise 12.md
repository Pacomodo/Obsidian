Give an example to show that a [[Factor ring|factor ring]] of an [[Integral Domain|integral domain]] may be a [[Field|field]].

___

**SOL**)
Consider an integral domain $\mathbb{Z}$.
Consider an ideal of $\mathbb{Z}$, $p\mathbb{Z}$, where $p$ is a prime.
Consider a factor ring $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, where isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$, which is field by [[Cor 19.12]].
