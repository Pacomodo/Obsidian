___

**DEF**)
Let a map $\phi : R \rightarrow R'$ be a [[Ring homomorphism|ring homomorphism]].
The subring $\phi ^{-1} [0']$ is the kernel of $\phi$, denoted by $Ker (\phi)$.

___

This $Ker(\phi)$ is the same as the [[대학 수업/대수학/책 정리/Section 13/Definition/Kernel|kernel]] of [[Group homomorphism|group homomorphism]] of $\left< R, + \right> \rightarrow \left< R', + \right>$.
