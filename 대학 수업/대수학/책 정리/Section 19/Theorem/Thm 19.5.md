___

**THEOREM**)
The cancellation laws hold in a [[Ring|ring]] $R$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $R$ has no [[Divisors of 0|divisors of 0]].

___

$(\Rightarrow)$ Suppose $ab = 0$ for some $a, b \in R$.
We need to show that either $a$ or $b$ is $0$.
If $a \neq 0$, then $ab = 0 = a0$, by cancellation law, $b = 0$.
Similarly, if $b \neq 0$, $a = 0$.

$(\Leftarrow )$ Suppose that $ab = ac$ with $a \neq 0$.
Then, $ab - ac = a(b-c) = 0$.
Since $a \neq 0$ and $R$ has no zero divisor, $b - c = 0$. so, $b = c$.
Similarly, if $ba = ca$ with $a \neq 0$, then $b = c$.
