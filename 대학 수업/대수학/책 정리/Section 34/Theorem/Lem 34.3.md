___

**LEMMA**)
Let $N$ be a [[Normal group|normal]] subgroup of a group $G$ and let $\gamma : G \rightarrow G/N$ be the canonical homomorphism.
Then the map $\phi$ from the set of normal groups of $G$ containing $N$ to the set of normal subgroups of $G/N$ given by $\phi(L) = \gamma[L]$ is one to one and onto.
