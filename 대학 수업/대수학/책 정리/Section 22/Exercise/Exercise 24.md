___

Prove that if $D$ is an [[Integral Domain|integral domain]], then $D[x]$ is an integral domain.

___

**PROOF**)
By [[Thm 22.2]], it is enough to show that for $D[x]$ have no [[Divisors of 0|zero divisor]].
Let $f(x) = a_nx^n + \cdots + a_0$ and $g(x) = b_mx^m + \cdots + b_0$ are in $D[x]$ and they are nonzero.
Because $D$ is integral domain, we know what $a_nb_m \neq 0$. Since the term of highest degree of $f(x)g(x) = a_nb_m$, $f(x)g(x) \neq 0$.
Hence, $D[x]$ is an integral domain.
