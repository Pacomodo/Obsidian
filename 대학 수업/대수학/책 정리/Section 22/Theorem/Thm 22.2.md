**THEOREM**)
Let $R$ be a [[Ring|ring]]. then the set $R[x]$, all polynomials in an indeterminate $x$ with coefficient in a ring $R$ is a ring under polynomial addition and multiplication.
If $R$ is commutative, then so is $R[x]$.
If $R$ has unity $1 \neq 0$, then $1$ is also unity for $R[x]$.
