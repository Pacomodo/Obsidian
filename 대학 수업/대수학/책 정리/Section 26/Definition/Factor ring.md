___

**DEF**)
The ring $R/N$ in [[Cor 26.14]] is the **factor ring (or quotient ring) of $R$ by $N$**.

___

**EXAMPLES**)

* Note that a factor ring of an integral domain may be a field.
See [[대학 수업/대수학/책 정리/Section 26/Exercise/Exercise 12]].

* Note that even though $R$ is not an integral domain, $R / N$ can be field.
See [[Exercise 14]].

* Note that a factor ring of a ring may be an integral domain, even though the original ring is not.
See [[Exercise 14]].

This examples shows that the structure of a factor ring are better than the original ring.

* Note that a factor ring of a integral domain may not be a integral domain.
See [[Exercise 13]].

This examples shows that the structure of a factor ring are worse than the original ring.
