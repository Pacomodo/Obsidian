___

**DEF**)
A nonconstant polynomial $f(x) \in F[x]$ is **irreducible over $F$** or is an **irreducible polynomial in $F[x]$** if $f(x) = g(x)h(x)$ for some $g(x), h(x) \in F[x]$, then either $g(x) \in F$ or $h(x) \in F$.

___

**EXAMPLES**)

* Let $f(x) \in F[x]$ and let $\deg(f(x)) = 2$ or $3$. Then $f(x)$ is irreducible over $F$ $\Leftrightarrow$ $f(\alpha) \neq 0$ for all $\alpha \in F$.
Go to [[Thm 23.10]].

* A polynomial $f(x)$ may be irreducible over $F$, but may reducible over $E$ where containing $F$.
$x^2 - 2 \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$, but reducible over $\mathbb{R}$.
