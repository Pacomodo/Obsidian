
___

**THEOREM**)
Let $R$ be a commutative [[Ring|ring]] with unity.
Then $R$ is a [[Field|field]] $\Leftrightarrow$ $R$ has no proper nontrivial [[Ideal|ideal]]s.

___

**PROOF**)

$(\Rightarrow)$ Go to [[Cor 27.6]].

$(\Leftarrow )$ If $R$ has no proper nontrivial [[Ideal|ideal]]s, then the only zero [[Ideal|ideal]] $\{0\}$ is [[Maximal ideal|maximal ideal]].
Then, $R/\{0\} \cong R$ is field by [[Thm 27.9]].
